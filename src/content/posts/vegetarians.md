Vegetarians are people who don't eat meat. They say is healthier and they think is better for the world because less animals are killed to eat. Vegetarians look for protein in other ways for example: soy meat, eggs, beans and more.

I disagree because I don't believe it's healthier. We need real proteins. In addition, real protein is 100% necessary to feel full. I don't believe a vegetarian diet is a balanced diet and healthier. One reason is we need real protein to grow muscle. For example, it's easier to get protein from whatever type of meat rather than soy meat.

As my second argument, it's easier and quicker to integrate real protein in your diet, to be more specific; prepare meat. You can cook a beef cut like ribeye, in 5 or 7 minutes. To illustrate: imagine you only have 30 minutes to prepare everything before you go to bed, so you have a little time to make dinner, hence you could have a beef cut in 7 minutes with potatoes.

Thoughts of some person about be vegetarians are for cultural beliefs, religion or ethic. For example: in Hinduism religion, people don’t eat cow, they think cows are sacred. In my opinion, I respect religious cultures but I don't share those ideas. Now, for cultural beliefs or for ethic, I don't really agree since protein is one of the most important means of nutrition we need.

To conclude, vegetarians are people who don't kill animals to eat, sometimes for cultural beliefs, religion, ethic or other thoughts. In my opinion, I don't agree with that because we find real protein in meat and when we eat meat we are not vegetarian and so we don't have a complete nutrition, we need meat in our diet.
