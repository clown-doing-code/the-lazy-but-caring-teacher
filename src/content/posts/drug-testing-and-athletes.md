All high school athletes should be drug tested. Athletes cannot use drugs when they are on a team because it is illegal in any sport. Many athletes are removed from their teams because drugs are found in their bodies. High school athletes should be drug tested because it protects their health and future, and it keeps sport fair.

First, drug testing protects athletes health and future. It is important for them because if they use drugs, 
they can lose their time, their carrer, and their opportunities. For example, many students lose scholarships and their place on the team for using drugs.

Second, drugs testing keeps sports fair and helps athlete stay on the team. When athletes are tested, they 
think twice before using drugs, so they stay clean and do not get removed. For example, a clean team can 
compete fairly and win honestly.

Some people say testing is expensive and invades privacy. However, health is more important than 
money. Nowadays tests are cheap and they can save a life.

In conclusion, high school athletes should be drug tested. In other words, testing high school athletes is 
necessary to keep them safe and to make sports honest. In my opinion, this is the best way to help students stay 
healthy and safe
