Homework can helps students learn. But too much homework can be a problema. It can cause streess and reduce free time. For these reasons, too much homework should banned.

Too much homework can make students feel stressed students already study many hours at school. For example, many assignments can make a students feel tired.

Students also need time for family, friends, and rest. For example, homework can leave Little time for these activities.

Some people think homework is important for learning. A small amout can help students practice. However, too much homework is not necessary.

In conclusión, The homework can be useful of learning. And my opinión however, too much can cause stress and reduce free time. Schools should give only necessary homework.
