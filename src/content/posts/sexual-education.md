Sexual education is very important for sexual health and avoid sexual illness. Know about sexual education can help you have a sexual life safety, and is necessary for all, no matter the age. Sexual education should be mandatory in schools because it helps prevent teenage pregnancy and reduces sexual illnesses among young people.

First, comprehensive sexual education helps students protect their health and prevent teenage pregnancy. When young people receive guidance in class, they learn how to make safe decisions for their life. One research tells us that one of four person active sexuality doesn’t know how to use preservative or don't want to use it. This shows that many teenagers need clear information in school to prevent problems.

Second, teaching sexual education in schools is necessary to avoid and reduce sexual illnesses. Many students do not know enough about protection or sexually transmitted infections. For instance, schools that offer these classes has lower rates of diseases because students understand how to protect themselves better.

On the other hand, some parents can be afraid about sexual education for their sons and/or daughters. They think "Why does my son/daughter need to know this information? Is just a kid, he/she doesn’t need to learn things like that". However, we need to know all about our body and how to keep healthy, no matter the age.
