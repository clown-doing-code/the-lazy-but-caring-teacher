At first glance, many people think school uniforms should be required, but in reality, they should
NOT be required because they are more expensive, they limit identity, and they don't solve anything.

First, they are more expensive for families. You have to buy normal clothes for going out and also the
uniform that is only sold in one expensive store. If it gets damaged, if it gets too small, or if you need to wash it,
you have to buy another one. It's a double expense that not all families can afford.

Second, they take away our identity and don't improve the school. Clothes are the way we show who we
are. Forcing us all to dress the same doesn't stop bullying, because people still judge you by your shoes, your
phone, or your hair. Discipline doesn't come from clothes, it comes from education.

For that reason, even though the title says they should be required, the reality is that they shouldn't be.
Schools should focus on teaching, not on controlling how we dress.
