Smoking is a big problem in the world. Smoking should be banned in all public places because it is bad for people's health and it pollutes the environment.

In the past, many people smoked in parks and streets. Today, we know that smoking causes serious illnesses like cancer. Many places do not allow smoking indoors, but people still smoke outside.

First, smoking in public harms non-smokers. When people smoke, others breathe the toxic smoke. This can cause heart and lung problems, especially in children. Clean air keeps everyone healthy.

Second, smoking causes trash and pollution. Many smokers throw cigarette butts on the ground. These butts have plastic and chemicals that hurt animals and water. Banning smoking keeps public places clean.

Some people say they have the freedom to smoke anywhere. However, personal freedom should not hurt other people. Breathing clean air is more important than smoking.

In conclusion, smoking should not be allowed in public places. This protects our health and
keeps the environment clean
