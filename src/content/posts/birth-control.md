Buying birth control without a prescription saves time and money and removes barriers for people without insurance or easy access to a clinic. It is backed by established safety, since it's a well-tested, widely used medication. Selling it over the counter follows the same model already working in other countries.

It eliminates the requirement for a physician's appointment, conserving both time and finances while reducing obstacles for individuals lacking insurance or residing at a distance from a healthcare facility. No doctor's visit needed means it's faster, cheaper, and more accessible for those without insurance or living far from a clinic. A woman without insurance wouldn't have to take the day off or pay for a doctor's visit. She could just go to the pharmacy and buy it.

Hormonal contraceptives have a prevalent history of being safe for the majority of individuals. Hormonal birth control has been used worldwide for decades and studies show it's safe for the vast majority of people, with side effects that are generally mild and manageable. For example, millions of women have taken the birth control pill for years without serious health issues, just occasional mild effects like nausea or headaches.

Some people disagree because they think women should consult a doctor before using birth control to make sure it is safe for them. I understand, but I think people should be able to buy birth control without a prescription. It is easier, saves time, and helps people who live far from a clinic.

Finally, birth control should be for sale over the counter because it saves people time and money and removes barriers for people without insurance or who live far from a clinic. Also hormonal birth control has been used for many years. I think women should have the right to buy birth control without needing a doctor's appointment.
