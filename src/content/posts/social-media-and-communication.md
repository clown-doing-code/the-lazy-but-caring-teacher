Social media is very popular today and almost everyone uses it. People use it to talk to friends, family and people from other countries. It is a fast way to communicate. However, I do not agree that it has improved human communication. In my opinion, social media has made communication more distant and less personal.

First, social media makes people more isolated, many people prefer to stay at home chatting online instead of meeting in person. For example, some people talk every day on WhatsApp or Instagram but they never visit their family. This happens because it is more comfortable to send a message than to have a real conversation.

In conclusion, I agree because social media is a good way to communicate with your friends and family, more easy and now you don't need to be in the same place. You can communicate with your family from another country and social media is a great way to investigate about all you need to know. However, people need to be more connected with the real world.
